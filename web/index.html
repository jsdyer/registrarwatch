Coverage & interpretation

¹ Totals sum the loaded rows before search filters. The built-in comparison follows eight selected IANA IDs. Loading an original CSV expands that quarter to the published registrar table. Its published comparisons cover generic top-level domains worldwide. Coverage depends on its feeds and inclusion criteria. An omitted registrar may fall outside the loaded selection.

Rates divide reported phishing domains by a registrar’s portfolio and multiply by 10,000. The same domain can appear in multiple quarters. These figures measure observed phishing, including on compromised domains. They do not establish registrar culpability.

What the data leaves open

The tables give no Canadian victim geography, referral dates, or confirmation of when a registrar acted. A registrar’s global rate cannot answer how long a reported Canadian impersonation site stayed active.

A focused CRTC request could seek existing aggregate counts of phishing-domain referrals to registrars and recorded disposition codes for one quarter.
